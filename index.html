A key distinction

IELTS policy isn't one-size-fits-all.

A university may accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or a conditional Medium of Instruction waiver — but rules can differ by programme, applicant background and previous education.

UniPath's intended approach: show the exact condition and official source, rather than labelling an entire university “IELTS-free”.
